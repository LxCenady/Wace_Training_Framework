# VC · Vector calculus & motion 向量微积分与运动 (Unit 3.3)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAS\topics\04_Vector_Calculus.md` / `.pdf` — 12 questions, 114 marks; calculator-assumed only (0 CF / 12 CA primary).

## Scope
- Position vector r(t); velocity v = r′, acceleration a = r″; integrate back with initial conditions.
- Cartesian equation of a path (eliminate t using trig identities or exponentials e^t ± e^−t → hyperbola, 2024 A18).
- Speed |v|, distance travelled ∫|v|dt (path length, incl. 3D curves 2023 A16), meaning of ∫v dt (displacement).
- Circular motion: angular speed, a parallel/antiparallel to r (2016S A19). (Ferris-wheel / merry-go-round questions 2017 A18, 2019 A18 have no vectors — they are related rates/SHM, see 06_Rates_DiffEq.)
- Projectile motion with gravity (and wind 2018 A19; ski jump 2021 A19).
- Straight-line motion in 3D with constant velocity: closest approach between two objects/to a fixed point, time within a distance (2017 A14 drone, 2024 A16 canyon wires).

## How SCSA asks it
- 9–16 mark multi-part CA questions: race car on a track (2017 A15, 2025 A12), particle on a closed curve (2016 A16, 2019 A13, 2022 A10), spiral path up a tower (2020 A12), drone on a hyperbola (2024 A18).

## Writing tips
- Include: initial position/velocity marked on a given path, a Cartesian-equation part, a speed or path-length part (numeric, "correct to 0.01"), and one interpretation.
- Keys: ✓ correct differentiation/integration, ✓ constant from initial condition, ✓ setup of integral for distance, ✓ value.
