# F · Functions & sketching graphs 函数与图像 (Unit 3.2)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAS\topics\02_Functions_Graphs.md` / `.pdf` — 31 questions, 220 marks; mostly calculator-free (21 CF / 7 CA primary).

## Scope
- Composite functions f∘g: rule, domain, range, existence conditions.
- One-to-one functions, inverse functions: rule, domain/range swap, graph reflected in y = x, domain restriction so an inverse exists; inverse trig (cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹) as in 2020 F6, 2024 F8.
- Absolute value: y = |f(x)|, y = f(|x|), solving |f(x)| = k, number-of-solutions functions N(k) (2020 A21), W-graphs (2024 A15).
- Reciprocal y = 1/f(x) from a graph of f.
- Rational functions: vertical/horizontal/oblique asymptotes, intercepts, holes; determine constants from a graph.

## How SCSA asks it
- CF: g∘f domain/range (2016 F1, 2018 F1, 2019 F4, 2022 F1); inverse sketch + rule (2017 F4, 2019 F5, 2021 F2, 2025 F2); 1/f(x) and f(|x|) sketches (2016 F8, 2020 F5, 2022 F2); constants of a rational function from its graph (2018 F4, 2020 F3, 2023 F1, 2024 F5 incl. oblique asymptote); sketch rational function (2017 F5, 2021 F4); f(|x|) and |f(x)| given, reconstruct f (2019 F7).
- CA: a|x − b| + c with four solutions (2016 A12), 1/f and f(−|x|) (2018 A14), rational f = k/q(x) symmetric about x = 3 (2022 A15), rational with oblique asymptote + area (2023 A18), absolute-value rule from a graph (2025 A9).

## Writing tips
- Sketch keys: asymptotes (each ✓), intercepts, turning points, correct behaviour near asymptotes, endpoints open/closed.
- Domain questions must have one subtle exclusion (e.g. x ≠ 1 from a zero denominator).
