# V · Vectors in 3D, lines, planes, spheres, linear systems 三维向量与线性方程组 (Unit 3.3)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAS\topics\03_Vectors_3D.md` / `.pdf` — 25 questions, 183 marks (11 CF / 12 CA primary).

## Scope
- Dot and cross products: angles, perpendicularity, normals, areas, parallelepiped volume (2018 F8).
- Lines (vector/parametric/Cartesian), planes (r·n = c, Cartesian, parametric r = a + λb + μc), spheres |r − c| = R and Cartesian form via completing the square.
- Intersections: line–plane, line–sphere, two lines (intersecting/skew), plane containing two lines; angle between line and plane; distance point–line (2016S A10), point–plane, between parallel planes (2019 A19); tangency.
- Vector proofs in geometry (2020 A20 square OACB, 2021 A16 prism, 2025 A18 pyramid).
- Systems of three linear equations: elimination, unique/none/infinitely many solutions depending on k, geometric interpretation (three planes meeting at a point, line, no common point).

## How SCSA asks it
- CF: solve 3×3 system then find k for no solution (2016 F6, 2018 F2, 2024 F6, 2025 F4); parallel planes check + solve + interpret (2020 F4, 2022 F5, 2023 F5 intersection line); sphere on diameter AB + plane through O (2016 F7); prism lines/planes (2017 F7); plane normal from parametric form (2020 F2).
- CA: sphere–line intersections (2016S A12, 2023 A9), laser reflection off a mirror plane (2016 A20), tangent sphere to a plane (2018 A17), normal/intersection/perpendicular plane/tangency (2019 A16, 2023 A14), ramp plane and closest point to a lamp (2022 A19), intersection of two lines and plane containing them (2025 A11), cinema ticket system (2021 A14).

## Writing tips
- CF systems: choose coefficients so elimination stays integer; include a "for which k" part and a geometric interpretation ✓.
- Keys: ✓ normal vector, ✓ substitution, ✓ correct point/equation; proofs ✓ each logical vector step + conclusion.
