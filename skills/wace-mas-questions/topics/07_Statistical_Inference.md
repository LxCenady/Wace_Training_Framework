Bank: `<ROOT>\MAS\topics\07_Statistical_Inference.md` / `.pdf` — 17 questions, 177 marks; calculator-assumed only (0 CF / 17 CA primary).

## Scope
- Sample mean X̄ of n observations: E(X̄) = μ, SD(X̄) = σ/√n; CLT makes X̄ approximately normal for n ≥ 30 regardless of parent (uniform, exponential, skewed dart distances).
- Sums of n variables: keys convert "total < T" to "X̄ < T/n" (2016 A19 total water, 2020 A18 total chocolate).
- P(a ≤ X̄ ≤ b), symmetric intervals P(|X̄ − μ| < d) = 0.96 ⇒ n.
- CI for μ: x̄ ± z·σ/√n (s replaces σ when unknown); recover x̄, s, n from a stated interval and width; sample size for a given margin.
- Interpretation: what 95% confidence means, true/false statements about intervals, comparing two intervals, claims ("everyone is 99% certain…", 2023 A15), quality-control decisions.
