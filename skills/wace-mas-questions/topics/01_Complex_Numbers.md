# C · Complex numbers 复数 (Unit 3.1)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAS\topics\01_Complex_Numbers.md` / `.pdf` — 43 questions, 264 marks (21 CF / 21 CA primary).

## Scope
- Cartesian/polar conversion, r cis θ, modulus/argument rules, conjugates, principal argument.
- De Moivre's theorem; powers; nth roots and roots of unity (plot on Argand diagram, regular polygons, product/sum of roots).
- Geometric meaning of multiplication by w (rotation by arg w, dilation by |w|); successive powers zwⁿ, quadrant questions.
- Loci and regions: |z − a| = r, |z − a| = |z − b|, Arg(z − a) = θ, Re/Im inequalities, |z − a| + |z − b| = k (ellipse, 2022 A9), z z̄ forms; max/min of |z − c| or arg z on a locus.
- Polynomials over C: factor theorem, conjugate root theorem, finding remaining roots, real coefficients from given complex roots.

## How SCSA asks it
- CF: a + bi arithmetic and (√3 − i)⁵ (2016 F2); factor/solve cubics & quartics given one complex root (2016 F3, 2017 F2, 2019 F2, 2021 F6, 2024 F7, 2023 F2 with unknown coefficients); roots of z⁴ = 2 − 2√3 i (2023 F6); z³ = ki from a plotted root (2025 F1); regular hexagon with vertex z₀ at the origin, not centred at O (2025 F7: |z₁…z₅| = 6r⁵ via cosine rule); Σ n iⁿ (2020 F8); z⁴³ = 1 vs z⁴⁷ = 1 (2021 F7); (a + bi)³ to solve a real system (2023 F8); arg(w) for w = z + iz̄ (2017 F1).
- CA: loci write/sketch (2016 A10, 2018 A11, 2019 A10, 2020 A10, 2021 A11, 2022 A9, 2024 A10, 2025 A10); powers as rotations (2017 A10, 2019 A12, 2023 A10, 2025 A19); roots of 2z⁶ = 1 + √3 i with first-quadrant count (2017 A19); z⁴ = −16i then arg(w + 2) (2016 A14); z²⁴ = 1 (2024 A9); (zⁿ − cis θ)(zⁿ + cis(−θ)) factorisation (2022 A18).
