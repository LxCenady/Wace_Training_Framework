# DE · Rates of change & differential equations 变化率与微分方程 (Unit 4.2)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAS\topics\06_Rates_DiffEq.md` / `.pdf` — 41 questions, 328 marks; nearly all calculator-assumed (2 CF / 28 CA primary).

## Scope
- Implicit differentiation: dy/dx, tangents, horizontal/vertical tangent points, second derivative (2025 F5).
- Related rates: tanks, cones, vases, hexagon area, lighthouse beam, shadows (chain rule with dV/dt).
- Slope fields: evaluate, identify DE from field, sketch solution curve.
- Separable DEs: general/particular solutions; exponential growth dP/dt = kP; logistic dP/dt = rP(1 − P/K) and solution P = K/(1 + Ae^(−rt)), inflection at K/2, max growth rate.
- Rectilinear motion with v as a function of x: a = v dv/dx = d(½v²)/dx, then x(t).
- Simple harmonic motion: ẍ = −n²x, x = A cos(nt + φ), v² = n²(A² − x²), period, amplitude, distance in a time interval; resisted motion (2024 A17).
