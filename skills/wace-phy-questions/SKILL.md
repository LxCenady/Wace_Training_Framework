# WACE Physics ATAR — original practice questions

Resolve ROOT from WACE_MATHS_ROOT or the ancestor containing PHY/pack.json. Read PHY/pack.json and tools/catalogue_PHY.json; use the topic guide below, the bilingual notes in PHY/methods and PHY/methods_en, and actual local marking-key crops.

The knowledge tree is the Year 12 syllabus: the 2026 and 2027 syllabuses have the same content, so each pattern is one syllabus point with both ids (S26-…, S27-…). Pattern OUT1 holds past-paper content no longer in the syllabus (mostly the Standard Model): never set it unless the user asks for it. The bank has 203 questions (2016–2025), 581 response parts and 1685 scoring criteria, each labelled on two axes by blind double labelling with adjudication: its syllabus pattern(s) and its award skill (what the mark is given for). Patterns marked "not yet examined" have no past question: write them from the syllabus point and the physics, without a past-paper shape to copy.

## Generation procedure

1. Choose the requested syllabus pattern. Follow the live blueprint below for marks, drawing requirements and question shapes; a pattern never examined takes the shape of a related examined pattern of its topic.
2. Read several local examples and the relevant year's formulae/data booklet. Produce a new context and numerical values; never reproduce or distribute SCSA text, figures or question crops.
3. Write the complete stimulus, explicit conventions, diagram or graph when required, and subpart marks. Diagram directions, field directions, axes, scales and units must be unambiguous.
4. Solve independently. Check dimensions, units, signs, vector directions, orders of magnitude, limiting cases and significant figures. Physics has no registered automatic numeric checker: do not claim SymPy verifies a physical model or diagram.
5. Write a marking rubric that sums to the printed maximum, with the balance of award skills real papers show for the pattern (its notes list them): e.g. a principle mark, working, the answer and a separate unit mark where papers give one. Preserve alternatives and any-N choices; state any cap explicitly. Label every criterion with its syllabus pattern(s) and one award skill.
6. Audit the question against the solution and rubric, revise discrepancies, and present the question before the answer unless the user requests otherwise.

## Sources and limitations

Section One Short response, Section Two Problem-solving and Section Three Comprehension have weights 30%, 50%, 20%; use each year's real raw totals. Reading time is 10 minutes, working time 180 minutes. Whole-paper weighted scores require every section.

The official online keys omit exact criteria for 2022 Q18(a), four marks, and 2025 Q19(f), three marks. Their labels are provisional, not recovered scoring standards; do not use them as exact rubric exemplars. Inspect key images when text extraction is incomplete.
