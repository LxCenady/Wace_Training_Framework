# EQT · Share capital, dividends and equity records 股本、股息与权益记录 (Units 3–4; current syllabus 2026–2027)

Source notes: `ACF/methods_en/07_Equity_Methods.md` (18 past-exam parts, 2020–2025). Catalogue: `tools/catalogue_ACF.json` (EQT1–EQT8). All eight EQT patterns are `examined: true`, so each has past questions. Past papers and marking keys: `ACF/papers/` (SCSA copyright; paraphrase only).

## Scope
- Share applications, allotment and the receipt calculation (EQT1).
- Ledger postings for bank, applications and ordinary share capital (EQT2).
- Share issue costs: payment and transfer against share capital where the stem requires it (EQT3).
- Bonus share issues: eligible shares, ratio, value, and reserve funding (EQT4).
- Declared and paid dividends: eligible shares, rate, declaration and payment entries (EQT5).
- Retained earnings ledger: opening balance, profit, dividends, reserve transfers, closing balance (EQT6).
- Statement of changes in equity (EQT7).
- Equity and dividend notes: share counts, issue prices, reserves, dividend rate and amount (EQT8).
- Excluded: historical items (OUT) and the cancelled 2021 item (RAT12) are not current content and are never generated here.

## How SCSA asks it
- Stem gives shares applied for or allotted, an issue price, dates, and sometimes a bonus ratio or dividend rate. Students compute a receipt or amount, then journalise, post, or present it.
- Ledger questions give opening balances and ask for postings and sometimes a balance. Balance only when asked.
- The statement of changes in equity is the largest equity item by marks (EQT7, 41 marks in the catalogue). It is built from the same movements as EQT1–EQT6, arranged by equity column.
- Notes (EQT8) reuse the figures from the statements. Every figure must agree with the statement it came from.
- "Is required" / "is not required" wording in the stem decides whether a transfer of issue costs is made (EQT3).
- Multiple-choice items test single effects: equity on dividend declaration, debit-side retained earnings entries, and the issue-cost entry.

## Method in brief
- **EQT1.** Receipt = shares applied for × issue price. Receipt on application: Dr Cash at bank, Cr Application. Allotment on its own date: Dr Application, Cr Ordinary share capital. Add a dated narration to each entry.
- **EQT2.** Enter opening balances on their correct side first. Post the receipt from the bank's view (Dr Bank, Cr Application). Post the allotment within the Application account and the matching credit in Ordinary share capital. Check that each posting appears on the other ledger. Balance only if asked.
- **EQT3.** Payment: Dr Share issue costs, Cr Cash at bank. If the stem says the costs are transferred, Dr Share capital, Cr Share issue costs. If it says not required, leave them. In an equity statement, show the costs as a reduction in share capital.
- **EQT4.** Shares eligible = existing shares (or paid-up capital ÷ issue price). Bonus shares = eligible shares × ratio. Value at the stated price. Transfer: Dr reserve or retained earnings, Cr Share capital. For "describe", name each source and say how it funds the issue.
- **EQT5.** Eligible shares = existing shares + bonus shares issued before the dividend date. Dividend = eligible shares × rate. Declaration: Dr Retained earnings, Cr Dividends payable. Payment: Dr Dividends payable, Cr Cash at bank.
- **EQT6.** Opening credit balance. Profit after tax on the credit side (a loss on the debit side). Dividends and transfers to the general reserve on the debit side; transfers from the general reserve on the credit side. Closing balance with Cr or Dr.
- **EQT7.** One column per item (share capital, general reserve, asset revaluation reserve, retained earnings, total). Enter openings, then cash issue and issue costs (share capital), revaluation gain (revaluation reserve), profit and dividends (retained earnings), transfers both ways, bonus issue (reserve to share capital). Total each column and check closing balances against the balance sheet.
- **EQT8.** Share capital note: each issue with shares and price, issue costs deducted from the issue they relate to. Reserves note: closing balances and general reserve additions. Dividends note: each paid or declared dividend with shares, rate and amount. Check every figure against the statement and balance sheet.

## Traps (examiners' reports and key notes)
- Account viewpoint (EQT2): a ledger entry is described from the account being completed. Do not reverse it by using the counterpart's view.
- Issue costs (EQT3): the key does not award a reversed entry (Dr Bank, Cr Share issue costs). Do not add a transfer when the stem says it is not required; do not omit it when it is.
- Expense treatment (EQT3): charging issue costs to expenses does not satisfy the specified equity treatment.
- Bonus share count (EQT4, EQT5): use the post-bonus share count for any dividend declared after the bonus issue. The pre-bonus count loses the dividend marks.
- Bonus valuation (EQT4): value the bonus shares at the stated price, not at market or another price.
- "Describe" versus "name" (EQT4): the key awards "identifies and describes". A bare label of a funding source does not earn the mark.
- Declaration versus payment (EQT5): a declaration does not touch the bank. Equity and retained earnings fall and liabilities rise on declaration.
- Recommended dividends (EQT5): a recommendation alone is not a current declaration entry. Only declared or paid dividends are current content.
- Revaluation (EQT7): the gain goes to the asset revaluation reserve, not to retained earnings.
- Transfers (EQT6, EQT7): transfers between equity components are not new income or new total equity. Check the sign of each transfer.
- Notes (EQT8): figures must match the statement, including the closing general reserve balance. Reserves are closing balances, not additions.

## Generator notes
- All eight EQT patterns have past questions. Do not invent a frequency beyond the note-level counts above.
- Build EQT7 items from two or three of EQT1–EQT6 movements, so that each movement is a separate award. Balance every column before publishing.
- For EQT1–EQT2, use a whole number of shares and an issue price in cents or whole dollars. Give a receipt and a dated narration requirement.
- For EQT4–EQT5, always make the bonus and dividend dates explicit, so the eligible share count is decidable from the stem.
- For the "is required / is not required" choice in EQT3, state the requirement in the stem; generate both variants only as separate questions.
- Verify before use: recompute every receipt, bonus count, dividend and closing balance; check that each equity column adds across; check that notes agree with the statement; confirm no OUT or RAT12 content appears.
