# FRM · Companies and the financial reporting framework 公司与财务报告框架 (Units 3–4)

Catalogue: `tools/catalogue_ACF.json` (FRM1–FRM9; all nine patterns have `examined: true`, so each has past-question evidence). Method notes: `ACF/methods_en/06_Framework_Methods.md` (based on 34 past-exam parts, 2020–2025, their keys and the examiners' reports). Keys: `ACF/` past papers and marking keys (copyrighted; paraphrase only).

## Scope
- Company forms: sole trader against company; public, large proprietary and proprietary companies. Separate legal entity, limited liability, membership limits, director minimums, share transfer, capital raising and the prospectus.
- Formation under the Corporations Act 2001; written constitution and replaceable rules.
- Reporting entities; external and internal users; the objective of general purpose financial reporting; fundamental and enhancing qualitative characteristics.
- Recognition of financial elements, the purposes of accounting standards, the accountant's role in business operations.
- Internal against external reporting; cash against accrual accounting.

## How SCSA asks it
- Compare two or three company types on named characteristics (FRM1). This is the largest FRM pattern by marks and years.
- Identify or explain one item of the framework: a reporting entity, an external user group, a fundamental characteristic, or a replaceable rule (FRM2–FRM4). Often a multiple-choice item.
- Decide whether an item meets the asset definition and recognition criteria (FRM5).
- Explain two or three purposes of accounting standards, with the stimulus cited (FRM6, a long-answer pattern with high mark weight per paper).
- Describe the accountant's role through three examples from different areas (FRM7).
- Compare internal and external reports, or cash and accrual reporting, with examples drawn from the stimulus (FRM8, FRM9).

## Pattern entries

### FRM1 · Explain company structures and capital-raising requirements 解释公司形式与筹资要求
- **Asks**: identify, compare or explain company characteristics across public, large proprietary and proprietary companies; advantages of a company over a sole trader; capital raising; the prospectus.
- **Method**: name the characteristic first, then set each company type side by side on it, then explain how and why they differ. For a prospectus, give its purpose and nature, then name the company type that must issue one.
- **Marks** (notes): two characteristics that change when a proprietary company becomes public, each explained; the separate legal entity and limited liability of shareholders; owner liability (unpaid share amounts limit liability equally); membership (no maximum for public companies, at most 50 non-employee members for large proprietary companies); directors (at least three for public, at least one for large proprietary); share transfer (unrestricted for public, subject to the constitution for proprietary); capital access (public companies may offer shares to the public, large proprietary may not); prospectus (4 marks for purpose and nature, 1 mark for the company type that must issue one).
- **Traps**: a characteristic with no contrast between company types; a comparison that covers only one type; a number (membership, directors) without naming the company type it applies to; describing a prospectus without its purpose; for advantages over a sole trader, the tested advantage is ease of transfer of ownership, not unlimited liability.
- **Not**: journal entries and share-application records belong to EQT. Prospectus requirements follow the scenario in the question, not a rule that every company must issue one.
- **Past questions** (notes): 2025 EA Q23(b); 2024 SA Q21(b); 2023 MC Q1; 2022 EA Q21(e); 2021 MC Q6; 2021 EA Q23(c); 2020 SA Q19(e).

### FRM2 · Explain company formation and internal rules 解释公司设立与内部规则
- **Asks**: how the Corporations Act 2001 governs formation; the purpose of a written constitution; how a company chooses a constitution, replaceable rules or both.
- **Method**: decide whether the question is about formation (the Act) or internal rules (constitution and replaceable rules). For formation, explain the legal requirements and their effect on the company. For a constitution, state what it sets out (rights and responsibilities of directors and shareholders, and internal management rules). For replaceable rules, say they are the Act's default set and apply where a company has no constitution of its own.
- **Marks** (notes): the Act's requirements for forming an Australian company, with their impact; how a company adopts a constitution and/or replaceable rules; the constitution's purpose; for multiple choice, replaceable rules as the default set.
- **Traps**: saying replaceable rules must be used by every company, or cannot be modified (both are distractors); explaining formation without referring to the Act.
- **Not**: identifying regulators belongs to GOV; insolvency procedures belong to GOV.
- **Past questions** (notes): 2025 EA Q23(c); 2023 MC Q8; 2021 SA Q19(d); 2020 MC Q2. See the discrepancy note below.

### FRM3 · Identify reporting entities and general-purpose reporting requirements 识别报告主体与通用财务报告要求
- **Asks**: external users; the objective of general purpose financial reporting; the nature of a reporting entity; which entities the Australian Accounting Standards apply to; differences in reporting requirements between reporting and non-reporting entities.
- **Method**: sort user groups inside or outside the entity (lenders, creditors and shareholders are external; managers, employees and directors are internal). State the objective as providing information useful for decision-making by users. Define a reporting entity as one that is required to, or chooses to, prepare general purpose financial reports. Match standards to entity type. For a comparison, describe the nature of a reporting entity, then explain each difference in full.
- **Marks** (notes): the objective with the two fundamental characteristics asked for (5 marks in total for that long answer); a reporting entity described, then two explained differences at the 3-mark level each; the principles and content of general purpose reports from the Conceptual Framework; for multiple choice, the external-user group, the objective, the entity definition or the standards scope.
- **Traps**: listing managers, employees or directors as external users; defining a reporting entity as a legal entity; saying ASIC administers the Conceptual Framework or that all companies must comply with it; giving a difference with no explanation of the reporting requirement; stating the objective as management-only or a budget focus.
- **Not**: the qualities of information belong to FRM4; preparing a particular statement belongs to STM or CSH.
- **Past questions** (notes): 2025 MC Q4; 2025 MC Q15; 2025 EA Q23(d); 2023 MC Q10; 2023 MC Q11; 2022 EA Q21(d); 2021 MC Q5; 2020 MC Q13.

### FRM4 · Apply qualitative characteristics of financial information 应用财务信息的质量特征
- **Asks**: identify, describe or apply fundamental and enhancing characteristics to the information in the stimulus.
- **Method**: fundamental characteristics are relevance and faithful representation; enhancing characteristics are comparability, verifiability, timeliness and understandability. For a definition, match the description to the characteristic (complete, neutral and free from error describes faithful representation). For a long answer, give the objective, then identify and outline two fundamental characteristics, each linked to the stimulus.
- **Marks** (notes): a correctly matched definition (1 mark); the four enhancing characteristics (1 mark); the long answer with the objective and two outlined fundamental characteristics (5 marks in total).
- **Traps**: putting enhancing characteristics in the fundamental set; treating timeliness or comparability as fundamental; naming a characteristic without outlining it.
- **Not**: an asset-definition or recognition argument is FRM5, not a list of qualities.
- **Past questions** (notes): 2025 MC Q3; 2022 MC Q12; 2022 EA Q21(d); 2020 MC Q7.

### FRM5 · Apply financial-element definitions and recognition 应用报表要素定义与确认条件
- **Asks**: whether an item should be recognised as an asset under the Conceptual Framework.
- **Method**: state the recognition criteria (the item meets the asset definition, and the information is relevant and reliably measurable). Test the item against the definition, then each criterion in turn. Give the decision and explain the criterion that fails, using relevance and reliable measurement.
- **Marks** (notes): for an item that is not recognised, an explanation of why it fails the criteria, applying relevance and reliable measurement (3 marks). For multiple choice, an asset must meet the definition, carry relevant information and be a faithful representation.
- **Traps**: stopping at the definition without the relevance and reliable-measurement tests; leaving the decision unexplained. Check any term against the framework the question names, because older terminology may differ.
- **Not**: routine balance-day arithmetic belongs to STM.
- **Past questions** (notes): 2024 SA Q19(e); 2021 MC Q1.

### FRM6 · Explain the purposes of accounting standards 解释会计准则的作用
- **Asks**: explain, discuss or outline a stated number of purposes of accounting standards, usually linked to investors, creditors, directors or the market.
- **Method**: give exactly the number of purposes asked for, each different. For each, state it, then explain the link (comparable reports, informed decisions, confidence in the market, a framework for directors). Use the stimulus to show why it matters in this scenario. For "discuss", weigh the purpose against the stimulus (for example, the benefit to investors against the directors' duty).
- **Marks** (notes): standards that give consistent, comparable reporting and confidence in capital markets; standards that give directors a framework and benchmark for accurate reporting; standards that support efficient capital markets, lower capital costs and international competitiveness; comparability between entities and across years for decisions by investors and creditors. Each purpose earns 2 marks when described and 1 mark when only identified.
- **Traps**: a one-line superficial point (1 mark, not 2); repeating one purpose in different words; ignoring the stimulus; discussing standards in general without linking to external users or directors.
- **Not**: a question about which statutory body performs a role belongs to GOV; calculating a statement item does not earn this pattern.
- **Past questions** (notes): 2024 EA Q22(d); 2021 EA Q23(e); 2020 SA Q19(d).

### FRM7 · Explain the accountant role in business operations 解释会计人员在企业经营中的职责
- **Asks**: describe three examples or functions of an accountant managing business operations, and sometimes explain the role.
- **Method**: choose three examples from different areas: (a) collecting, maintaining and checking financial information; (b) analysing information, advising management and monitoring controls; (c) meeting reporting, tax, GST and regulatory duties. For each, say what the accountant does and why it matters to running the business.
- **Marks** (notes): one relevant example from each area at 2 marks each; an explanation of the role at 3 marks; for functions, three identified at 1 mark each.
- **Traps**: three examples from one area; general statements such as "the accountant does the accounts"; no link to the stimulus; no accounting terms (accuracy of records, internal controls, GST, tax reporting).
- **Not**: an employee complying with a regulation is not the regulator; naming ASIC or another reporting body belongs to GOV.
- **Past questions** (notes): 2023 EA Q22(e); 2020 EA Q21(f).

### FRM8 · Compare internal and external reporting 比较内部与外部报告要求
- **Asks**: describe or state differences between internal and external reporting for a company, usually in relation to the types of report.
- **Method**: choose distinct differences across report type, format, accounting standards, timeliness, audit requirements and scope. For each, state what applies to internal and to external reports. For a described difference, add one clause of explanation (for example, who uses the report and why this changes its type). For a question addressed to a named company's management, tie the difference to that company's reports.
- **Marks** (notes): 1 mark for each stated difference and 2 marks for each described difference; each must be a contrast between the two kinds of report.
- **Traps**: the same feature given for both kinds of report; a difference stated without saying how the two differ; a difference outside the listed areas that is not linked to report types.
- **Not**: a simple identification of management versus financial accounting is FIN1; a cash/accrual comparison is FRM9.
- **Past questions** (notes): 2023 SA Q18(d); 2022 SA Q20(c). See the discrepancy note below.

### FRM9 · Distinguish cash and accrual accounting 区分现金制与权责发生制
- **Asks**: explain the difference between cash and accrual performance reporting, using the company's performance reports, and give examples.
- **Method**: define cash accounting (income and expenses recorded when cash is received or paid). Define accrual accounting (income earned and expenses incurred in the period are recorded whether or not cash has moved). Explain the timing difference using the stimulus. Give two examples from the reports: for instance, income earned but not yet received, and expenses incurred but not yet paid, each tied to the named company.
- **Marks** (notes): the explanation of the difference (2 marks); two examples from the company's reports (2 marks). For multiple choice, accrual profit is based on income earned and expenses incurred in the period.
- **Traps**: saying accrual profit is always larger or smaller than cash profit (both are distractors); examples not drawn from the reports; defining accrual as cash receipts and payments.
- **Not**: applying an adjustment while preparing a budget is BUD; preparing a statement adjustment is STM.
- **Past questions** (notes): 2024 MC Q7; 2020 EA Q22(d).

## Traps across the topic (examiners' reports and the methods notes)
- **Facts without the stimulus.** Tie every answer to the company, entity or scenario in the question.
- **Naming without explaining.** Each identified item usually earns a separate explanation mark. A bare list of characteristics, purposes or differences loses most of its marks.
- **Superficial long answers.** A one-line point is capped at the superficial mark (1 mark for a purpose in FRM6).
- **Terminology.** Use the course terms: separate legal entity, limited liability, qualitative characteristics, cash and accrual, reporting entity.
- **Unanswered later parts.** Attempt every part of an extended question.

## Generator notes
- Build a stimulus for FRM1 with a fictional company that is changing type (for example, a proprietary company planning to go public), then ask for two changes and their explanations. Keep the thresholds exact: public companies have no membership maximum; large proprietary companies have at most 50 non-employee members; the director minimums are three and one.
- For FRM3 and FRM6, use a short scenario with named user groups; keep the objective statement and the "reporting entity" definition accurate, and never attribute the Conceptual Framework to a regulator.
- For FRM5, pick an item that fails exactly one criterion (for example, relevance or reliable measurement) so the explanation has one clear point.
- For FRM8 and FRM9, build two report extracts from one invented company, so that each difference or example can be checked against them.
- Keep FRM questions separate from GOV (regulators, insolvency, audit) and from EQT (share records). Do not pull in OUT content or RAT12 material; neither is current content.
- Verify before publishing: company type and thresholds; the fundamental and enhancing lists; the asset-recognition tests; the mark split (for example 2 + 2 + 1); every example drawn from the stimulus.
