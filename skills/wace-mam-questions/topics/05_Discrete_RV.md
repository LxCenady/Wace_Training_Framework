# DRV · Discrete random variables & binomial 离散随机变量与二项分布 (Unit 3.3)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAM\topics\05_Discrete_RV.md` / `.pdf` — 27 questions, 284 marks (4 CF / 14 CA primary).

## Scope
- Probability tables; Σp = 1; E(X), Var(X), SD; cumulative P(X ≤ x); conditional P(X = 1 | X ≤ 3).
- Linear transformations E(aX + b), Var(aX + b) — scaling marks, profit = price × X − cost.
- Bernoulli; binomial Bin(n, p): mean np, variance np(1 − p), n and p from mean/SD, P(X ≥ k), assumptions: keys reward "independent trials" and "same/constant p" (2019 A18(b), 2020 A12(h)); fixed n and two outcomes are usually given, not assumptions.
- Games of chance: expected winnings, fair game, charge needed for a target profit.

## How SCSA asks it
- Fundraiser games: dice sums (2017 A13), Lucky 7 scratchie (2021 A10), Lucky Cup dice (2022 A9), carnival buckets (2021 A13), Mr Ulam's dice game (2024 A14), balls without replacement (2025 A8).
- Table completion with unknowns from E(X) (2016S A14), reconstructing lost survey data (2019 A10), cumulative table (2024 F3).
- Binomial in context: alarm system works if ≥ 2 alarms work (2019 A18), robot forward/back moves (2025 A15), n and p from mean and SD (2016S F4), spinner (2020 F1), marbles with replacement (2018 F1).
- Often merged with normal ("of 20 parcels, at least half weigh more than 3.7 kg") or with CIs.

## Writing tips
- Include a "State the distribution of X, including parameters" ✓ and an assumptions/justification part.
- Money-context expected values should end with a decision ("Should the school run this game?").
