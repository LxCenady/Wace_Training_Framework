## Scope
- Relative-frequency histograms → probabilities (2017 F1, 2020 F4, 2023 F3).
- pdf conditions (≥ 0, total area 1), constants, P(a < X < b), cumulative distribution, median, E(X) = ∫x f(x)dx, Var(X) = ∫x² f(x)dx − μ².
- Uniform distribution: keys award marks for stating the pdf with its domain and writing Var as an integral (2024 F4(a), 2019 F6(e), 2016 A16(b)); (b − a)²/12 is NOT on the formula sheet — use it only to check. Triangular/piecewise-linear pdfs from graphs. Given a cdf F(x): P(a<X≤b) = F(b) − F(a), pdf = F′(x) (2023 A11(d)(e)).
- Normal: probabilities, inverse normal, z = (x − μ)/σ, μ and σ from two percentages, 68–95–99.7 rule (CF), suitability of a normal model from a histogram.
- Conditional probabilities; combining with binomial for repeated independent trials; linear transformation of units (°C → °F).
