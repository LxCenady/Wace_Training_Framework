# I · Integration & applications 积分及其应用 (Unit 3.2; ∫1/x from 4.1)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAM\topics\02_Integration.md` / `.pdf` — 39 questions, 317 marks. Strongly calculator-free (16 CF / 7 CA as primary).

## Syllabus scope
- Antiderivatives of xⁿ, e^(ax+b), sin, cos, 1/x, f′(x)/f(x), (ax+b)ⁿ; linearity; finding f from f′ and a point.
- "Hence" integration using a derivative just found (d/dx(x ln x) ⇒ ∫ln x; d/dx(x sin 3x) ⇒ ∫x cos 3x).
- Definite integrals: signed area, from graphs made of segments/arcs; ∫ₐᵇ f′(x)dx = f(b) − f(a).
- Fundamental theorem: d/dx ∫ₐˣ f(t)dt = f(x), chain-rule variants, sketching A(x) = ∫ f(t)dt.
- Area under a curve and between curves; total change from a rate.
- Riemann sums: upper/lower rectangle estimates and why they improve (2017 F9, 2024 F6).

## How SCSA asks it
- Integrals read from a graph of lines/arcs (2016S F1, 2016 F7, 2022 F2) — exact answers with π.
- Area between eˣ and lines, exact answers with ln (2016 F6, 2017 F5, 2019 F5, 2020 A11).
- FTC sketch/interpret: A(x) = ∫₋₁ˣ f(t)dt from a graph (2016 F5, 2016S A20); sign of F(x) = ∫₀ˣ t sin t dt (2025 F2).
- Table puzzle with f, f′, f″, F (2023 F5); area between parabola and line, explain translation invariance (2021 F5).
- CA applications: cross-sections of buildings/trench/dam/bottle (2021 A9, 2022 A7, 2023 A9, 2023 A14, 2024 A16).

## Writing tips
- CF integrals must evaluate exactly; prefer limits like 0, ln 2, π/6.
- Include a graph-only integral (segments + quarter circles) at least once per set.
- Key: separate ✓ for correct antiderivative, substituting limits, final exact value.
