# L · Exponential & logarithmic functions 指数与对数函数 (Units 3.1, 4.1)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAM\topics\04_Exp_Log.md` / `.pdf` — 42 questions, 347 marks (10 CF / 17 CA primary).

## Scope
- Log laws, change of base, exact solution of exponential/log equations (incl. quadratics in eˣ).
- Graphs of y = logₐ x and transformations: asymptote, intercepts, finding m, p, q in y = m logₐ(x − p) + q from points.
- ln x as the inverse of eˣ; derivative of ln f(x); ∫ f′/f.
- Growth/decay models P = P₀eᵏᵗ: initial value, k from half-life/doubling, time to reach a level, rate at a time.
- Logarithmic scales: decibels L = 10 log(I/I₀), Richter, site rank, moment magnitude — "how many times greater".
- Log-linear data: ln(population) linear in time (2019 A17).

## How SCSA asks it
- CF: express ln 6, ln 6.25 in p = ln 2, q = ln 3, r = ln 5 (2023 F2); log₁₀2 = x, log₁₀7 = y (2017 F7); solve 4e²ˣ = 81 − 5e²ˣ (2017 F3); 2 ln x = ln 9 + 4 and change of base in m, n (2025 F7); read a log graph to solve (2019 F4, 2022 F4, 2024 F5).
- CA models (7–13 marks): drug concentration (2018 A9, 2024 A8), half-life (2016 A9, 2016S A10), animal colony (2017 A16), oven T₀ − 175e^(−0.07t) (2020 A15), light intensity (2022 A14), bee colony (2023 A6), apple mass (2025 A9), stopping distance s = 40.6 ln(0.15D) (2025 A11), Rubik's algorithms b log₄(t+1)+c (2024 A17).
- Log scales: Richter (2016 A12), decibels (2016S A13, 2018 A18, 2025 A17), moment magnitude graph (2024 A15).

## Writing tips
- CF answers exact (ln(3/2) etc.); CA: "correct to …" plus an interpretation part.
- Strong mixed question: log model + derivative (rate) + inverse (time to level) + interpretation.
