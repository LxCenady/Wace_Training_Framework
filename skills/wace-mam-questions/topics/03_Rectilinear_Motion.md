# M · Rectilinear motion 直线运动 (Units 3.1–3.2)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAM\topics\03_Rectilinear_Motion.md` / `.pdf` — 13 questions, 111 marks; mostly calculator-assumed (3 CF / 9 CA).

## Scope
- x(t) → v = x′ → a = v′, and back with initial conditions (constants from given positions/velocities).
- At rest (v = 0), changes direction (v changes sign), speeding up vs slowing down (signs of v and a), maximum speed.
- Displacement ∫v dt vs total distance ∫|v| dt (split at rest times); "during the fifth second" = t ∈ [4, 5].
- Interpretation of ∫ₐᵇ v dt (change in position).

## How SCSA asks it
- 8–12 mark contextual CA question: elevator v = (9π/16) sin(πt/16) (2019 A9), sprinter with exponentials (2022 A11), damped spring 3e⁻ᵗ sin t (2022 A10), oscillating mass (2023 A8), cable car (2021 A17), model train a = pt − 13 with two position conditions (2017 A20), drone (2018 A11).
- CF versions: x = 5 sin 3t (2016 F4); cubic advertising graphic with a position table, total distance (2024 F2); sign table of x, v, a to interpret (2025 F6).
- Short CA: "how far has the boat travelled before its acceleration is zero" (2021 A14).

## Writing tips
- Include a "speeding up or slowing down? Justify" part — keys require reference to both signs (a negative alone is not justification).
- Total-distance keys: ✓ rest times, ✓ correct piecewise expression, ✓ value.
