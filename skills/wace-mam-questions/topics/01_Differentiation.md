# D · Differentiation & applications 微分及其应用 (Unit 3.1)

Bank: `<ROOT>\MAM\topics\01_Differentiation.md` / `.pdf` — 51 questions (incl. secondary tags), 413 marks. Both sections almost equally (22 CF / 21 CA as primary).

## Syllabus scope
- Derivatives of eˣ, eᵏˣ, sin, cos, tan (via quotient rule), ln x, ln f(x); product, quotient, chain rules and combinations.
- Second derivative, concavity, points of inflection; first/second derivative tests.
- Curve sketching from a rule, or from sign/derivative information (sign tables, conditions like f′(−1)=0, f″(−1)>0).
- Optimisation in context ("show that" the objective function, then maximise/minimise, justify nature, answer in context).
- Rates of change, tangents/normals, increments formula δy ≈ f′(x)δx, marginal cost/profit.

## How SCSA asks it (2016–2025)
- CF opener (4–9 marks): "Differentiate …" with e, ln, trig; "Determine f″(x). Do not simplify." (2016S F6, 2018 F3, 2021 F1, 2024 F1).
- Table-of-values product/chain rule: given g, h, g′, h′ at x = 1, 2, 3, evaluate (gh)′ and (h∘g)′ (2019 F2; from graphs 2020 F5).
- Constants from conditions: cubic with turning point/inflection/area (2018 A15, 2020 F3); sketch from conditions only (2022 F5).
- Full analysis 10–15 marks: show f′ = …, stationary points + nature, inflection points, sketch with features (2016 A13 x² ln x, 2017 A14 x ln(x+3), 2020 F7 e²ˣ − 4eˣ, 2021 A12 x²eˣ).
- Optimisation 6–12 marks with a "show that" setup: ladder round a corner (2016S A19), triangle in a circle (2016 F8), can surface area (2017 A17), fencing (2020 A17), log sideboards (2024 A12), vase of decorations (2019 A16). Keys reward: derivative, solve = 0, justify max/min (sign test or f″), answer with units.
- Increments formula (2–3 marks): triangle area (2016 A11), ln(2.02) (2021 F3), marginal profit (2019 F7).
- Interpretation: "Explain the meaning of your answer" in context (2017 F6, 2019 F1).

## Writing tips
- CF: choose functions whose stationary points land on nice x (ln 2, π/3, integers).
- Always include a justification part for the nature of stationary points (worth a ✓).
- Sketch keys: intercepts, stationary points, inflection, asymptotic/end behaviour, overall shape — one ✓ each.
