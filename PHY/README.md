# Physics ATAR pack — syllabus content and award skills

This pack contains the 2016–2025 past-question catalogue, labelled against the 2026/2027 Year 12 syllabus.
It uses two axes: 78 syllabus content descriptors plus OUT1 for content outside the current syllabus, and
11 award skills (SKL.K1–K11, AO1–AO3). Past questions outside the current syllabus remain identifiable;
new content without past questions is explicitly marked unexamined. The old 65-pattern classification
and its draft review are not the current pack.

Physics uses one shared paper: Short response (30%, suggested 50 minutes), Problem-solving (50%, 90 minutes),
and Comprehension (20%, 40 minutes), with 10 minutes reading and 180 minutes working. Raw marks vary each year;
the three section percentages determine the examination score. Formulae and Data booklets remain separate resources.

There are 203 questions, 581 response parts and 1,685 labelled scoring criteria. Bilingual notes cover all
79 content nodes, grouped into the ten topics in pack.json. The question-generation skill uses these
content nodes and award skills; OUT1 is not used for new practice unless the user requests it.

The 2026 and 2027 syllabus content descriptors were compared and mapped to shared identifiers. Original
exam/key PDFs and extracted question text stay in ignored local folders and are not distributed. The pack
contains source URLs and hashes, classification inputs, original notes and a generation skill.

Keys use Description/Marks tables, sometimes with an Element column. The pack uses the built-in
description-marks handler and reviewed scoring splits. Numeric checking remains disabled because
units-aware checking is not implemented. Figures, vector diagrams, graphs and comprehension passages
retain references to the user's locally obtained originals.

Known discrepancies: 2016 Q16 has a 13-mark exam heading and 14-mark key heading, but key parts total 13;
2021 Q18 has a 15-mark exam heading and 14-mark key heading, but key parts total 15. Use the exam/part totals
and preserve the official PDFs, recording the inconsistent headings in `papers.acknowledged`.

The pack uses the post-2.0.1 reviewed-split override fix: a checked scoring shape takes precedence even
when the parser happened to reproduce the total. The finished pack therefore requires WTF 2.0.2 or newer;
this version requirement is independent of the framework's release schedule.

Visual review identified copyrighted omissions in the keys, including 2022 Q18(a) and 2025 Q19(f).
Their exact scoring criteria are unavailable in the official online files. They must be recorded as unresolved,
not replaced by invented criteria or described as drawings. A matching mark total alone is not semantic validation.

Independent blind labels agreed on award skill at 91.7%, shared syllabus content at 89.4% (identical sets
75.6%), and in/out-of-syllabus status at 98.6%; 515 disputed criteria were adjudicated. These are agreement
figures, not guarantees of correctness. The 63 single-adjudicator replacements and a few inquiry labels
remain review priorities; see methods/审校记录.md. Teachers should review generated material.

An isolated export/import/rebuild trial matched all nine checked database tables. Source classification and
notes are ready for this workflow, with the above limitations retained. The old classification review and
legacy cheat sheets are excluded; the current pack's reference resource is the official formulae/data booklet.
